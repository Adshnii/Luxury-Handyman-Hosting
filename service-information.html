Service information

Luxury Handyman Company is the public brand of Solvix.S LLC, serving Naples, Bonita Springs and Marco Island, Florida.

Scope of work

We provide minor home repairs, hanging and mounting, routine maintenance and furniture assembly within the qualifications and permits required for each job. We do not offer electrical wiring, plumbing connections or other work requiring a trade license. Requests are reviewed individually before any work is accepted.

Inquiries, estimates and appointments

The website is an invitation to discuss your project. Submitting a message does not book a visit, establish a service contract or confirm a price. The scope, price, materials, timing and any applicable permit requirements must be agreed before work starts. Changes to the work should be agreed separately.

Pricing depends on the scope and estimated labor. Clients cover agreed material costs; labor and materials are itemized on the invoice. Project-specific payment, cancellation and other terms belong in the written estimate or agreement.

Availability and accessibility

Working hours are Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–6:00 PM and Sunday 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Eastern Time. Saturday is closed; visits are by arrangement. You may send a request at any time. This form is not an emergency response service.

If you have difficulty using the website or form, call +1 (239) 776-0639 or email info@luxuryhandymansolvix.com for assistance.
