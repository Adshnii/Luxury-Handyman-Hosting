THE WAY WE WORK

We work as if it were
our own home.

Our approach is simple: we work as if it were our own home. We take the time to follow the instructions, check the fittings and give the finished piece the attention it deserves. If a part is missing or damaged, we let you know and discuss the next step.

You stay informed as the work moves forward, and we remain available after assembly. If you have a question about the work or need help with another item, we are here for you.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A little planning.
A thoughtful result.

Share the furniture brand or model, the number of items and your location. Let us know whether the boxes are already in the room and whether instructions and hardware are available.

Assembly is subject to the item’s condition, supplied parts and manufacturer’s requirements. Required anti-tip anchoring is discussed before work begins; built-in cabinetry, electrical connections and structural modifications are not included.

What if a part is missing or damaged?

We will let you know before continuing with the affected step and discuss what is needed to complete the assembly.

How is the price confirmed?

We discuss the scope, estimated time and materials before the work starts. You receive an invoice showing labor and materials; materials used for the job are paid by the client.

How do I arrange a visit?

Call or message us on WhatsApp with your location, the work needed and your preferred date. Visits are arranged individually. Working hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Eastern Time. Saturday is closed.
