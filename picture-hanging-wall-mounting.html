YOUR VISIT, STEP BY STEP

Know what to expect.

The number of pieces, their size and weight, wall material, mounting height and the layout all affect the work involved. A single framed print and a carefully spaced gallery wall need different preparation. Tell us what you have so we can discuss the scope and price before work begins.

  1. Tell us what needs attention

    Share the number of pieces, approximate dimensions and weight, and the wall material if known. For a TV, include its model and the bracket details. Let us know about high positions, stairs or restricted access.

  2. Agree the details

    We discuss the scope, materials, price and timing before work begins. Materials used for the job are paid by the client and shown on the invoice alongside labor.

  3. Stay informed throughout

    We keep you updated at each stage and remain available for questions after the work is finished. For a condo or managed property, let us know about access, parking, elevator reservations or permitted work hours when arranging the visit.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Questions before
your visit.

Mounting depends on the wall condition, fixing points and the manufacturer’s instructions. Electrical wiring, in-wall cable work and structural alterations are not included.

Can you help with the layout?

Yes. We can discuss height, spacing and alignment with you before fixing items to the wall.

How do I arrange a visit?

Call or message us on WhatsApp with your location, the work needed and your preferred date. Visits are arranged individually. Working hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Eastern Time. Saturday is closed.

Can you hang a gallery wall or several pictures?

Yes. We discuss the arrangement, spacing and height with you before fixing the pieces. Include the number and sizes of the pictures when you contact us so the layout work can be considered in the estimate.

Can you mount a heavy mirror or a TV?

We first check the item, wall condition, suitable fixing points and mounting hardware. The installation must be appropriate for the weight and the manufacturer’s requirements. In-wall wiring and electrical work are not included.

Do I need to buy brackets or fixings first?

Tell us what hardware you already have. We can discuss and purchase suitable materials for the agreed work; the client pays for materials, which are shown on the invoice alongside labor.
