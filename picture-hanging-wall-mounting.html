BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A little planning.
A thoughtful result.

Tell us what you would like to hang, how many items there are, their approximate size and weight, and the type of wall if you know it. For TVs, include the model and bracket details.

Mounting depends on the wall condition, fixing points and the manufacturer’s instructions. Electrical wiring, in-wall cable work and structural alterations are not included.

Can you help with the layout?

Yes. We can discuss height, spacing and alignment with you before fixing items to the wall.

How is the price confirmed?

We discuss the scope, estimated time and materials before the work starts. You receive an invoice showing labor and materials; materials used for the job are paid by the client.

How do I arrange a visit?

Call or message us on WhatsApp with your location, the work needed and your preferred date. Visits are arranged individually. Working hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Eastern Time. Saturday is closed.
