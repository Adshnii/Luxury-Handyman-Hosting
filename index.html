A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we get started.

What should I include in my request?

Please include your location, the type and scope of work you need, and your preferred start date and time. Let us know the address where the work will take place.

How is the price confirmed?

The price depends on the scope of work and the estimated time needed to complete it. Our prices are fair and affordable. The client covers the cost of all materials used for the job. You don’t need to buy them yourself—we take care of purchasing them. When the work is complete, you receive an invoice showing the charges for labor and materials.

When can I contact you?

Contact us whenever it’s convenient for you—we’re available to our clients 24/7. Our working hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Eastern Time. Saturday is our day off. Visits are arranged individually.
