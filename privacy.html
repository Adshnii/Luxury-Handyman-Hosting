Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, city or service location, email address and project description. We use this information to reply, discuss the requested work and arrange next steps. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing or confirm an appointment. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, government identification or other sensitive information through this form.

How messages are delivered

The current form prepares a message on your device and opens your chosen email application. It does not send a message from this website or submit the form to a server. The prepared message contains a greeting to our company and the project text you enter. You review and send it through your email provider. Until you send it, we do not receive it. Our business mailbox is provided by Spacemail by Spaceship. Messages are not stored in our public GitHub repository.

We have not installed advertising trackers, analytics scripts or first-party tracking cookies. Linked external services operate under their own privacy policies.

Following Facebook or WhatsApp links opens services operated by Meta. Using an email link opens your chosen email application. Those providers process information according to their own policies.
